IT IS AGREED as follows.


ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1  In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following
expressions have the following meanings.

"Affiliate" means, in relation to a Party, any entity that directly or
indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that
Party, where "control" means the ownership of more than fifty percent (50%)
of the voting share capital.

"Applicable Laws" means all laws, statutes, regulations, binding codes of
practice and regulatory rules applicable to a Party in any jurisdiction in
which it performs its obligations under this Agreement, including the UK
GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, the Financial Services and Markets Act
2000, and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday on
which clearing banks are ordinarily open for business in both London and
Bengaluru.

"Charges" means the fees payable by the Customer to the Supplier as set out
in Schedule 3, as adjusted in accordance with Clause 9.

"Confidential Information" means all information of a confidential nature
disclosed (whether in writing, orally or by any other means) by one Party to
another Party, including the terms of this Agreement, but excluding
information which: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through
breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in the receiving Party's
possession before disclosure; or (c) is independently developed by the
receiving Party without reference to the disclosing Party's information.

"Customer Data" means all data, records and materials provided by or on
behalf of the Customer to the Supplier, or generated by the Services on the
Customer's behalf.

"Deliverable" means any output, report, software or other work product to be
delivered by the Supplier under a Statement of Work.

"Good Industry Practice" means the exercise of that degree of skill,
diligence and foresight which would reasonably be expected from a skilled and
experienced supplier engaged in the same type of undertaking.

"Insolvency Event" has the meaning given in Clause 23.4.

"Personal Data" shall have the meaning given to it in the UK GDPR.

"Regulatory Authority" means the Financial Conduct Authority, the Prudential
Regulation Authority, the Information Commissioner's Office, or any successor
or equivalent body in any relevant jurisdiction.

"Service Levels" means the performance standards set out in Schedule 2.

"Services" means the services described in Schedule 1 and in each Statement
of Work.

"Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a document executed by the Parties in the
form set out in Schedule 5 describing services to be provided.

"Term" has the meaning given in Clause 4.1.

1.2  Clause and Schedule headings do not affect the interpretation of this
Agreement.

1.3  A reference to a Clause or Schedule is a reference to a Clause of or a
Schedule to this Agreement.

1.4  Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa.

1.5  Any phrase introduced by the words "including", "includes", "in
particular" or "for example" is illustrative and does not limit the sense of
the words preceding those terms.

1.6  In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the documents
forming this Agreement, the order of precedence shall be: (a) the Clauses of
this Agreement; (b) the Schedules; and (c) any Statement of Work, save that a
Statement of Work may vary the Charges applicable to the services described
in it where it expressly states an intention to do so.


ARTICLE 2 - STRUCTURE OF THE AGREEMENT

2.1  This Agreement sets out the framework under which the Supplier will
provide the Services. It does not itself oblige the Customer to procure any
particular volume of services.

2.2  Each Statement of Work forms a separate contract incorporating the terms
of this Agreement by reference.

2.3  Schedules 1 to 7 form part of this Agreement and are incorporated by
reference into it.


ARTICLE 3 - SUPPLY OF SERVICES

3.1  The Services shall be performed by the Supplier with reasonable skill
and care and in accordance with Good Industry Practice, Applicable Laws and
the Service Levels.

3.2  The Supplier will procure that all personnel engaged in the performance
of the Services hold the qualifications and clearances specified in
Schedule 4.

3.3  The Supplier is to notify the Customer promptly upon becoming aware of
any circumstance likely to prevent or materially delay the performance of the
Services.

3.4  Where a Deliverable is subject to acceptance testing, the Customer shall
notify the Supplier of acceptance or rejection within ten (10) Business Days
of delivery. If the Customer does not respond within that period the
Deliverable shall be deemed accepted.

3.5  Nothing in Clause 3.4 obliges the Customer to accept a Deliverable which
does not materially conform to the specification in the relevant Statement of
Work.


ARTICLE 4 - TERM AND RENEWAL

4.1  This Agreement commences on 1 April 2025 and, unless terminated earlier
in accordance with its terms, continues until 31 March 2028 (the "Initial
Term").

4.2  Upon expiry of the Initial Term this Agreement shall continue thereafter
for consecutive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "Renewal Term") unless
either Party serves written notice of non-renewal not less than one hundred
and eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term.

4.3  The Initial Term and any Renewal Terms are together the "Term".


ARTICLE 5 - CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES

5.1  The Customer shall provide the Supplier with such access to its
premises, systems and personnel as is reasonably necessary for the
performance of the Services.

5.2  The Customer shall appoint a relationship manager who is to act as the
primary point of contact for the Supplier.

5.3  The Supplier shall not be liable for any failure to meet a Service Level
to the extent that failure is caused by the Customer's failure to comply with
Clause 5.1, provided the Supplier notifies the Customer of the dependency
failure within five (5) Business Days of becoming aware of it.


ARTICLE 6 - CHARGES AND PAYMENT

6.1  In consideration of the provision of the Services, the Customer shall
pay the Charges set out in Schedule 3.

6.2  The annual base charge for the Initial Term is INR 4,80,00,000 (Rupees
Four Crore Eighty Lakh) per annum, invoiced quarterly in arrears.

6.3  Charges relating to services delivered from the United Kingdom shall be
invoiced in pounds sterling at a rate of GBP 1,150 per person-day.

6.4  The Supplier shall issue invoices monthly. The Customer shall pay each
correctly rendered and undisputed invoice no later than the tenth (10th)
Business Day of the month following the month in which the invoice is
received.

6.5  If the Customer disputes an invoice in good faith it shall notify the
Supplier within fifteen (15) Business Days of receipt, pay the undisputed
portion in accordance with Clause 6.4, and the Parties shall resolve the
dispute in accordance with Clause 29.

6.6  Interest on overdue undisputed amounts shall accrue at the rate of two
percent (2%) per annum above the Bank of England base rate from time to time,
accruing daily from the due date until payment in full.

6.7  Any failure by the Customer to pay an undisputed invoice within sixty
(60) days of the due date shall constitute a material breach of this
Agreement for the purposes of Clause 23.2.

6.8  All Charges are exclusive of value added tax, goods and services tax and
any similar taxes, which shall be payable by the Customer in addition at the
applicable rate.


ARTICLE 7 - TAXES AND WITHHOLDING

7.1  Each Party is responsible for its own taxes on its own income.

7.2  If the Customer is required by Applicable Laws to withhold or deduct any
amount from a payment, it shall pay such additional amount as ensures that
the Supplier receives the amount it would have received had no withholding
been required.

7.3  The Supplier shall provide a valid tax residency certificate within
thirty (30) days of a written request by the Customer.


ARTICLE 8 - SERVICE LEVELS AND SERVICE CREDITS

8.1  The Supplier shall meet or exceed the Service Levels set out in
Schedule 2.

8.2  Where the Supplier fails to meet a Service Level:

8.2.1  the Supplier shall pay or credit to the Customer the service credits
calculated in accordance with Schedule 2; and

8.2.2  the Supplier shall, within ten (10) Business Days, provide the
Customer with a root cause analysis and a remediation plan.

8.3  Service credits are the Customer's sole financial remedy for a failure
to meet a Service Level, save that this Clause 8.3 does not limit the
Customer's rights under Clause 23.3 or its right to claim for breach of
Clause 15 or Clause 16.

8.4  If the Supplier fails to meet the same Service Level in three (3)
consecutive months, the Customer may terminate this Agreement or the affected
Statement of Work on thirty (30) days' written notice.


ARTICLE 9 - CHANGE CONTROL AND INDEXATION

9.1  Neither Party shall be obliged to accept a change to the Services except
in accordance with the change control procedure in Schedule 6.

9.2  The Supplier may increase the Charges once in each calendar year with
effect from 1 April, by an amount not exceeding the increase in the UK
Consumer Prices Index over the preceding twelve (12) months, on giving not
less than ninety (90) days' written notice.

9.3  No change to this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and
signed by an authorised representative of each Party.


ARTICLE 10 - PERSONNEL AND NON-SOLICITATION

10.1  The Supplier shall not remove or replace any person designated as key
personnel in a Statement of Work without the Customer's prior written
consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

10.2  During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months following its
expiry or termination, neither Party shall knowingly solicit for employment
any employee of the other Party who has been materially involved in the
provision or receipt of the Services.

10.3  Clause 10.2 does not prohibit the making of a general recruitment
advertisement not specifically targeted at the other Party's employees.


ARTICLE 11 - SUBCONTRACTING

11.1  The Supplier may subcontract the performance of any part of the
Services to an Affiliate without the Customer's consent.

11.2  The Supplier shall not subcontract to any third party which is not an
Affiliate without the prior written consent of the Customer.

11.3  The Supplier remains fully responsible for the acts and omissions of
its subcontractors as if they were its own.


ARTICLE 12 - INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

12.1  All intellectual property rights owned by a Party before the date of
this Agreement, or created independently of it, remain vested in that Party.

12.2  All intellectual property rights in the Deliverables created
specifically for the Customer under a Statement of Work shall vest in the
Customer upon payment in full of the Charges relating to them.

12.3  The Supplier grants the Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive,
royalty-free licence to use any pre-existing materials of the Supplier
embedded in a Deliverable to the extent necessary for the Customer to enjoy
the benefit of that Deliverable.

12.4  The Customer retains all right, title and interest in the Customer
Data. The Supplier is granted a limited licence to process Customer Data
solely for the purpose of providing the Services.


ARTICLE 13 - AUDIT AND RECORDS

13.1  The Supplier shall maintain complete and accurate records relating to
the Services and the Charges for a period of seven (7) years.

13.2  The Customer and any Regulatory Authority may audit those records on
not less than ten (10) Business Days' written notice, save that no notice is
required where the audit is required by a Regulatory Authority or where the
Customer reasonably suspects fraud.

13.3  Audits shall be conducted no more than twice in any twelve (12) month
period unless a material breach has been identified.


ARTICLE 14 - COMPLIANCE AND ANTI-BRIBERY

14.1  Each Party shall comply with all Applicable Laws relating to
anti-bribery and anti-corruption, including the UK Bribery Act 2010 and the
Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.

14.2  Each Party shall maintain policies and procedures designed to prevent
any breach of Clause 14.1 and shall enforce them where appropriate.

14.3  Breach of Clause 14.1 shall be deemed a material breach of this
Agreement which is not capable of remedy.


ARTICLE 15 - CONFIDENTIALITY

15.1  Each Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of
every other Party and shall not disclose it except to those of its officers,
employees, professional advisers and subcontractors who need to know it for
the purposes of this Agreement and who are bound by obligations of
confidentiality no less onerous than those in this Article 15.

15.2  The obligations in Clause 15.1 continue for a period of seven (7) years
following the expiry or termination of this Agreement.

15.3  Clause 15.1 does not prevent disclosure required by Applicable Laws, by
a court of competent jurisdiction or by a Regulatory Authority, provided that
the disclosing Party gives the other Party as much notice as is reasonably
practicable and lawful.

15.4  Each Party acknowledges that damages may not be an adequate remedy for
breach of this Article 15 and that the other Party is entitled to seek
injunctive relief.


ARTICLE 16 - DATA PROTECTION

16.1  For the purposes of the UK GDPR, the Customer is the controller and the
Supplier is the processor in respect of Personal Data processed under this
Agreement.

16.2  The Supplier shall process Personal Data only on the documented
instructions of the Customer and in accordance with the data processing terms
set out in Schedule 4.

16.3  The Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay and in any
event within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of any personal data
breach affecting Customer Data.

16.4  The Supplier shall not transfer Personal Data outside the United
Kingdom or the European Economic Area without the Customer's prior written
consent and the implementation of an appropriate transfer mechanism.

16.5  Upon expiry or termination of this Agreement the Supplier shall, at the
Customer's election, return or securely delete all Personal Data within
ninety (90) days, save to the extent retention is required by Applicable
Laws.


ARTICLE 17 - INFORMATION SECURITY

17.1  The Supplier shall implement and maintain the technical and
organisational security measures set out in Schedule 4.

17.2  The Supplier shall procure an independent penetration test of the
systems used to provide the Services at least once in every twelve (12) month
period and shall provide the summary report to the Customer within twenty
(20) Business Days of receipt.

17.3  The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any security incident
affecting the Services within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it.


ARTICLE 18 - WARRANTIES AND REPRESENTATIONS

18.1  Each Party warrants that it has full power and authority to enter into
and perform this Agreement.

18.2  The Supplier warrants that: (a) the Services will be performed in
accordance with Clause 3.1; (b) the Deliverables will materially conform to
the specifications in the relevant Statement of Work for a period of ninety
(90) days following acceptance; and (c) the Deliverables will not infringe
the intellectual property rights of any third party.

18.3  Except as expressly set out in this Agreement, all warranties,
conditions and terms implied by statute or common law are excluded to the
fullest extent permitted by Applicable Laws.


ARTICLE 19 - INDEMNITIES

19.1  The Supplier shall indemnify and hold harmless the Customer against all
losses, damages, costs and expenses arising out of any claim that the
Services or the Deliverables infringe the intellectual property rights of a
third party.

19.2  The indemnity in Clause 19.1 does not apply to the extent the claim
arises from: (a) the Customer's modification of a Deliverable; (b) use of a
Deliverable in combination with materials not supplied by the Supplier where
the infringement would not have arisen but for that combination; or (c)
Customer Data.

19.3  The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against any fine or penalty
imposed on the Customer by a Regulatory Authority to the extent it arises
from the Supplier's breach of Article 16.

19.4  The Customer shall indemnify the Supplier against any claim arising
from the Supplier's use of Customer Data in accordance with the Customer's
documented instructions.

19.5  A Party seeking to rely on an indemnity in this Article 19 shall notify
the indemnifying Party of the relevant claim within twenty (20) Business Days
of becoming aware of it, and shall not settle the claim without the
indemnifying Party's consent.


ARTICLE 20 - LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

20.1  Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes the liability of any Party
for: (a) death or personal injury caused by its negligence; (b) fraud or
fraudulent misrepresentation; (c) any liability which cannot lawfully be
limited or excluded.

20.2  Subject to Clause 20.1, no Party shall be liable to another for any
loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of anticipated savings, loss of
business opportunity, or any indirect or consequential loss, in each case
whether arising in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise.

20.3  Subject to Clauses 20.1 and 20.2, the total aggregate liability of each
Party arising out of or in connection with this Agreement in any period of
twelve (12) months shall not exceed the greater of (a) INR 6,00,00,000
(Rupees Six Crore) and (b) one hundred and twenty five percent (125%) of the
Charges paid or payable in that period.

20.4  The limit in Clause 20.3 does not apply to: (a) the Customer's
obligation to pay the Charges; (b) the Supplier's indemnities under Clauses
19.1 and 19.3; or (c) either Party's breach of Article 15.

20.5  Each Party shall take reasonable steps to mitigate any loss it suffers.


ARTICLE 21 - INSURANCE

21.1  The Supplier shall maintain, with a reputable insurer, professional
indemnity insurance of not less than GBP 10,000,000 per claim and cyber
liability insurance of not less than GBP 5,000,000 in the aggregate.

21.2  The Supplier shall provide evidence of such insurance within ten (10)
Business Days of a written request.


ARTICLE 22 - FORCE MAJEURE

22.1  No Party is liable for any failure or delay in performing its
obligations to the extent caused by an event beyond its reasonable control,
provided it notifies the other Parties promptly and uses all reasonable
endeavours to mitigate the effect of the event.

22.2  Clause 22.1 does not excuse the Customer's obligation to pay Charges
for Services actually received.

22.3  If a force majeure event continues for a period of more than ninety
(90) days, either affected Party may terminate this Agreement by written
notice without further liability.


ARTICLE 23 - TERMINATION

23.1  The Supplier may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving not
less than three hundred and sixty five (365) days' written notice to the
Customer.

23.2  Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice
if another Party commits a material breach of this Agreement which is capable
of remedy and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of written notice
specifying the breach and requiring it to be remedied.

23.3  Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice
if another Party commits a material breach which is not capable of remedy.

23.4  Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice
if another Party suffers an Insolvency Event, meaning that it becomes unable
to pay its debts as they fall due, enters administration, liquidation or any
analogous procedure in any jurisdiction, or has a receiver or administrative
receiver appointed over the whole or any material part of its assets.

23.5  The Customer may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice
if the Supplier undergoes a change of control in favour of a competitor of
the Customer.

23.6  The Customer may terminate any Statement of Work on sixty (60) days'
written notice without terminating this Agreement.


ARTICLE 24 - CONSEQUENCES OF TERMINATION

24.1  On expiry or termination of this Agreement the Customer shall pay all
Charges accrued up to the effective date of termination.

24.2  The Supplier shall provide exit assistance in accordance with
Schedule 7 for a period of up to one hundred and eighty (180) days following
the effective date of termination, at the rates set out in Schedule 3.

24.3  The Supplier shall return or securely delete all Customer Data in
accordance with Clause 16.5.

24.4  Termination of this Agreement automatically terminates every Statement
of Work then in force, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.


ARTICLE 25 - SUSPENSION

25.1  The Supplier may suspend the Services in whole or in part where the
Customer has failed to pay an undisputed invoice which remains outstanding
more than ninety (90) days after the due date.

25.2  The Supplier shall give the Customer not less than twenty (20) Business
Days' written notice before exercising its right under Clause 25.1.

25.3  The Supplier may suspend the Services immediately and without notice
where continued provision would, in its reasonable opinion, create a material
security risk or breach Applicable Laws.


ARTICLE 26 - ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

26.1  No Party may assign, transfer or charge any of its rights or
obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the
other Parties, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

26.2  Notwithstanding Clause 26.1, either Party may assign this Agreement to
an Affiliate or to a purchaser of the whole or substantially the whole of its
business on written notice.

26.3  Each Party shall notify the others in writing within ten (10) Business
Days of any change of control affecting it.


ARTICLE 27 - NOTICES

27.1  Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by
hand, by pre-paid recorded delivery or by email to the address specified in
Schedule 5.

27.2  A notice delivered by hand is deemed received at the time of delivery;
a notice sent by recorded delivery is deemed received on the second Business
Day after posting; a notice sent by email is deemed received at the time of
transmission, provided that if transmission occurs after 5.00 pm on a
Business Day it is deemed received on the next Business Day.

27.3  A notice of termination or of a material breach may not be given solely
by email.


ARTICLE 28 - ANTI-FACILITATION AND MODERN SLAVERY

28.1  The Supplier shall not engage in any practice constituting an offence
under the Modern Slavery Act 2015 and shall have due regard to the
prohibition of forced labour under Applicable Laws.

28.2  The Supplier shall, within thirty (30) days of a written request,
provide the Customer with details of the steps taken to comply with
Clause 28.1.


ARTICLE 29 - DISPUTE RESOLUTION

29.1  Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall
first be referred to the relationship managers of the Parties, who shall meet
within ten (10) Business Days of the referral and use reasonable endeavours
to resolve it.

29.2  If the dispute is not resolved within twenty (20) Business Days of the
referral under Clause 29.1, it shall be escalated to a director of each
Party, who shall meet within a further ten (10) Business Days.

29.3  If the dispute remains unresolved twenty (20) Business Days after
escalation under Clause 29.2, it shall be finally resolved by arbitration
administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre in accordance
with the SIAC Rules, before a tribunal of three (3) arbitrators, seated in
Singapore, in the English language.

29.4  Nothing in this Article 29 prevents a Party from applying to the courts
of England and Wales for interim or injunctive relief in respect of a breach
of Article 15 or Article 12.


ARTICLE 30 - GOVERNING LAW

30.1  This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection
with it or its subject matter are governed by and construed in accordance
with the laws of England and Wales.


ARTICLE 31 - GUARANTEE

31.1  The Guarantor unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to the
Customer the due and punctual performance by the Supplier of all of its
obligations under this Agreement.

31.2  The Guarantor's liability under Clause 31.1 is subject to the same
limitations as apply to the Supplier under Article 20.

31.3  The guarantee in this Article 31 is a continuing guarantee and remains
in force until all obligations of the Supplier under this Agreement have been
discharged in full.


ARTICLE 32 - GENERAL

32.1  This Agreement, together with the Schedules and each Statement of Work,
constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior
discussions, correspondence, negotiations and arrangements relating to its
subject matter.

32.2  No failure or delay by a Party in exercising any right under this
Agreement constitutes a waiver of that right.

32.3  If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or
unenforceable, that provision shall be severed and the remainder shall
continue in full force and effect.

32.4  Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or
relationship of employer and employee between the Parties.

32.5  A person who is not a Party has no right under the Contracts (Rights of
Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any term of this Agreement, save that an
Affiliate of the Customer receiving the Services may enforce Article 20.

32.6  This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an
original and which together constitute one agreement.

32.7  Articles 1, 12, 15, 16, 19, 20, 24, 29, 30, 31 and 32 survive the
expiry or termination of this Agreement, together with any other provision
expressed to survive it.
