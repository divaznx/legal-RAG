This Amended and Restated Master Services Agreement supersedes the Master

the undersigned client.

Client: Jonathan Meyers

Contact email: j.meyers@example-corp.com

Clause 1. Services
The Provider shall deliver the consulting services described in Exhibit A,
as amended by Change Order CO-2025-07. Exhibit A and all executed Change
Orders are incorporated by reference.

Clause 2. Fees and Payment
The Client shall pay a monthly retainer of USD 15,000, due within
forty-five (45) days of invoice. Late payments accrue interest at 1.0% per
month.

Clause 3. Term
This Agreement commences on the Restatement Date and continues for an
initial term of twenty-four (24) months. Renewal is NOT automatic: the
Agreement renews only by written amendment signed by both parties.

Clause 4. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60)
days' prior written notice. Either party may terminate immediately upon a
material breach that remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written
notice of the breach. The Provider may additionally suspend services if any
invoice remains unpaid for more than sixty (60) days.

Clause 5. Confidentiality
Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information with at
least reasonable care. Confidentiality obligations survive termination for
five (5) years.

Clause 6. Limitation of Liability
Neither party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall exceed two
times (2x) the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the
claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential
damages, except for breaches of Clause 5 (Confidentiality).

Clause 7. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
