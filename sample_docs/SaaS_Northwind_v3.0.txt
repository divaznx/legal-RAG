SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT

Clause 1. Definitions and Interpretation
1.1 "Authorised User" means an individual employee or contractor of the
Customer who has been issued credentials to access the Platform.
1.2 "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one party
to the other that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to
be confidential, but excludes information that is or becomes public through
no breach of this Agreement.
1.3 "Platform" means the hosted software service described in Schedule 1.
1.4 "Personal Data" has the meaning given in the UK GDPR.
1.5 "Service Credit" means the credit calculated in accordance with Schedule 2
and applied against future Subscription Fees.
1.6 "Subscription Fees" means the recurring charges set out in Schedule 3.
1.7 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

Clause 2. Grant of Access
2.1 Subject to Clause 3 and the Customer's payment of the Subscription Fees,
the Supplier grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to
permit Authorised Users to access the Platform during the Term.
2.2 The Customer shall not sublicense, resell, or provide access to the
Platform to any third party except as expressly permitted in Clause 14.

Clause 3. Customer Obligations
3.1 The Customer shall ensure that all Authorised Users comply with the
acceptable use policy set out in Schedule 4, which is incorporated by
reference into this Agreement.
3.2 The Customer shall not attempt to reverse engineer, decompile, or derive
the source code of the Platform.
3.3 Breach of Clause 3.2 constitutes a material breach that is not capable of
cure for the purposes of Clause 11.

Clause 4. Subscription Fees and Payment
4.1 The Customer shall pay Subscription Fees of GBP 96,000 per annum, invoiced
annually in advance and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.
4.2 Overdue amounts accrue interest at four percent (4%) per annum above the
Bank of England base rate, accruing daily from the due date until payment.
4.3 The Supplier may suspend access to the Platform under Clause 10 if any
undisputed invoice remains unpaid for more than forty-five (45) days.
4.4 All amounts are exclusive of VAT, which the Customer shall pay in addition
at the applicable rate.

Clause 5. Service Levels
5.1 The Supplier shall provide the Platform with a monthly uptime of at least
99.5%, measured in accordance with Schedule 2.
5.2 If uptime falls below the level in Clause 5.1, the Customer's sole
financial remedy is the Service Credit, save that this Clause 5.2 does not
limit the Customer's termination right under Clause 11.4.

Clause 6. Confidentiality
6.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret
and shall not disclose it except to those of its personnel who need to know it
for the purposes of this Agreement.
6.2 The obligations in Clause 6.1 continue for five (5) years after
termination or expiry of this Agreement.
6.3 Nothing in this Clause 6 prevents disclosure required by law or by a
competent regulatory authority, provided the disclosing party gives prompt
written notice where lawful to do so.

Clause 7. Data Protection
7.1 The Supplier processes Personal Data as processor and the Customer acts as
controller. The parties shall comply with the data processing terms set out in
Schedule 5.
7.2 The Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any
event within seventy-two (72) hours, of becoming aware of a personal data
breach affecting the Customer's Personal Data.
7.3 The Supplier shall not transfer Personal Data outside the United Kingdom
without the Customer's prior written consent.

Clause 8. Intellectual Property
8.1 All intellectual property rights in the Platform remain vested in the
Supplier. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of those rights.
8.2 The Customer retains all intellectual property rights in data it uploads
to the Platform and grants the Supplier a licence to process that data solely
to provide the Platform.
8.3 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third party claims that
the Platform infringes a UK patent or copyright, subject to Clause 12 and to
the Customer notifying the Supplier promptly of any such claim.

Clause 9. Warranties
9.1 The Supplier warrants that it will provide the Platform with reasonable
skill and care.
9.2 Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, all warranties, conditions,
and terms implied by statute or common law are excluded to the fullest extent
permitted by law.

Clause 10. Suspension
10.1 The Supplier may suspend access to the Platform where required under
Clause 4.3, or where continued access presents a material security risk.
10.2 The Supplier shall give the Customer at least five (5) business days'
written notice before suspending access under Clause 10.1, except where an
immediate suspension is necessary to prevent harm.

Clause 11. Term and Termination
11.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an
initial term of thirty-six (36) months (the "Initial Term"), and thereafter
renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives not
less than ninety (90) days' written notice before the end of the then-current
term.
11.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice
if the other party commits a material breach which is capable of cure and
fails to cure it within thirty (30) days of written notice specifying the
breach.
11.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice
if the other party becomes insolvent, enters administration, or has a receiver
appointed over any of its assets.
11.4 The Customer may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written
notice if monthly uptime falls below 99.5% in any three (3) consecutive
months.
11.5 On termination the Customer shall pay all Subscription Fees accrued up to
the effective date of termination, and the Supplier shall make the Customer's
data available for export for thirty (30) days.

Clause 12. Limitation of Liability
12.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits either party's liability for death or
personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for any liability that
cannot lawfully be limited.
12.2 Subject to Clause 12.1, neither party is liable for loss of profit, loss
of business, loss of anticipated savings, or any indirect or consequential
loss.
12.3 Subject to Clauses 12.1 and 12.2, each party's total aggregate liability
arising under this Agreement is limited to one hundred and fifty percent
(150%) of the Subscription Fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the
event giving rise to the claim.
12.4 The limit in Clause 12.3 does not apply to the Customer's obligation to
pay Subscription Fees, or to the Supplier's indemnity under Clause 8.3.

Clause 13. Force Majeure
13.1 Neither party is liable for failure to perform caused by an event beyond
its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other party promptly and uses
reasonable endeavours to mitigate the effect.
13.2 If a force majeure event continues for more than sixty (60) days, either
party may terminate this Agreement on written notice without liability.

Clause 14. Assignment and Subcontracting
14.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior
written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.
14.2 Either party may assign this Agreement to an affiliate or to a purchaser
of substantially all of its assets on written notice.
14.3 The Supplier may subcontract performance but remains responsible for its
subcontractors' acts and omissions.

Clause 15. Survival
15.1 Clauses 1, 6, 8, 12, 15, 16, and 17 survive termination or expiry of this
Agreement, together with any provision expressed to survive.

Clause 16. Governing Law
16.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws
of England and Wales.

Clause 17. Dispute Resolution
17.1 The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute by escalation to
their respective senior executives within fifteen (15) business days.
17.2 If the dispute is not resolved under Clause 17.1, it shall be finally
resolved by arbitration under the LCIA Rules, seated in London, before a sole
arbitrator.
17.3 Nothing in this Clause 17 prevents either party from seeking injunctive
relief from the courts of England and Wales in respect of a breach of
Clause 6 or Clause 8.

Clause 18. Entire Agreement
18.1 This Agreement, together with Schedules 1 to 5, constitutes the entire
agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.
18.2 In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and a Schedule, the
terms of this Agreement prevail.
